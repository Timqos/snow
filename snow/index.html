Snow changes the instrument model: projects and sounds from other firmware may not translate. Keep the backup for rollback.

INSIDE SNOW

Made for hands-on sound.

One role. Many voices.

Kick · Snare · Hat · Percussion · Bass · Chord. Each channel starts with ten factory sounds and stays its own instrument. Go much further with three oscillators, FM pair/chain/parallel, ring, additive and fold algorithms; sine, triangle, saw, square, pulse and organ waves.

Full amp ADSR, pitch/FM envelopes, filters, two LFOs and modulation are available from 27 EDIT pages. Percussion can play pitched notes; bass follows your line, and chords keep their polyphonic body.

A new sound, with a backbone.

On the first EDIT page, OCT+ creates a fresh instrument sound. It coordinates oscillator ratios, register, envelopes, filtering, modulation and effects instead of throwing every control to an unrelated extreme.

Chords stay playable chords. Bass keeps its low end. Drums keep their attack and body. Sounds are generated from a large parameter space, rather than cycled through the factory presets. Hold SAVE to undo.

Patterns that keep moving.

GEN RND creates new rhythm, notes and velocities. GEN PARM adds new parameter locks to the existing notes. PARM AMT controls their amount and depth; CLEAR removes steps and locks.

Each role gets its own phrasing. Musical influences mix inside the generator without a genre menu. Pitched parts respect the scale, and hats/percussion can add rare retriggers and triplets. EVOLVE refreshes phrases as they play.

Space for every channel.

Independent delay, reverb and chorus settings and tails on all six channels. Tape, bitcrush, drive and insert effects add colour. Smart sound generation keeps the dry instrument in front and avoids piling up extreme processing.

A pixel interface, ten system colours and value-linked icons keep the instrument readable. HOME, MIXER and PATTERNS always show all six channels together.

FIRST FIVE MINUTES

Start on the device above.

  1. Switch on the simulator. Drag an encoder up/down, use the piano keys, and press the hardware-style buttons. Shift-click holds a button down.
  2. Tap SEQ, then turn SELECT to SEQ GEN. TARGET initially names your selected channel. Turn K1 to ALL for a complete groove.
  3. Press OCT+ to generate notes and rhythm, then PLAY/STOP. Press OCT+ again for a new phrase. OCT− generates parameter locks; K4 sets PARM AMT.
  4. Tap EDIT. On its first MACROS page, OCT+ generates a new sound for the selected instrument. Hold SAVE to undo. Turn ALGORITHM to choose another channel.

The simulator runs the firmware’s C synthesis and interface through WebAssembly. Browser saves stay on this computer. It does not emulate the physical USB audio, MIDI output or TRS ports.

QUICK REFERENCE

Under your hands.

HOME
Return to six channel cards and the scope. Each icon reflects the position of the last parameter you turned. Hold HOME for settings, including the display colour.
ALGORITHM / PRESETS / SELECT
Normally: choose channel / browse that instrument’s factory sounds / move through pages. The current EDIT page and parameter names tell you what K1–K4 control.
EDIT → MACROS → OCT+
Generate a new sound across all EDIT pages of the selected channel. Instrument identity, sequence and other channels stay intact. Hold SAVE for undo. OCT+ on other EDIT pages keeps its normal action.
SEQ → SEQ GEN
OCT+: GEN RND. OCT−: GEN PARM. SAVE + OCT−: CLEAR. K1: selected channel / ALL; K2: density; K3: variation; K4: parameter-lock amount. SELECT opens the remaining generation pages.
HOLD SEQ
All six pattern columns. K1–K4 select the first four channels’ patterns; ALGORITHM selects bass patterns and PRESETS chord patterns. OCT± switches A–H / I–P. Black keys select a channel; white keys select its pattern. Launches are quantized during playback.
HOLD GLO
Six channel levels: K1–K4, ALGORITHM and PRESETS. The first six black keys mute; the first six white keys solo while held. E4: unmute all. F4: fill. G4: tap tempo. GLO + SELECT sets tempo; GLO + OCT− restores the mixer snapshot.
FX / ENV / LFO / SEL
Open effects, envelopes, modulation and scale/chord settings. EDIT also exposes the detailed sound and per-channel FX pages. Manual controls retain their full ranges.
SAVE / TEMP SAVE
Use SAVE’s PROJECT pages to store a project. GLO + SAVE takes a temporary RAM snapshot; GLO + HOME reloads it, and GLO + OCT+ reloads the selected channel. Temporary snapshots disappear after power-off.
CONTROL ALL
GLO + EDIT + K1–K4 changes the included channels together. GLO + EDIT + OCT− toggles the selected channel’s inclusion. A sound generated by RANDOM EDIT keeps its role; manual editing can still take it far beyond that starting point.

Up to 64 steps per pattern, 16 pattern positions per channel and a shared pool of 64 unique patterns. The deferred-save queue is finite; stop playback if the device asks for space to save.

Open source, in progress.

Snow builds on Skiff and Felucca. The firmware and simulator share the same sound engine. Development and software testing continue; hardware validation is still required.
