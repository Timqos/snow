STATTIC

Four instruments on the M-VAVE FM-1: sampled drums, an FM bass and two FM keys voices. STATTIC generators for sequences, sounds and parameter locks, and a five-page HOME with a large pixel interface.

A candidate for testing on hardware. Builds and host tests cannot prove click-free audio on a particular FM-1. The USB console shows each channel's processing time and a counter of missed audio buffers.

In this build

  • SY and CH: a two-operator FM keys voice (electric pianos, organs, glassy keys), 15 presets each.
  • BS: 15 presets, five of them built on the sine wave.
  • DR and BS share one effects rack; SY and CH keep their own.
  • HOME 2 on BS: K4 makes new parameter locks on the same notes. On DR, K1–K4 make kick, snare, hat and percussion with their own locks.
  • Drum generator: half-time and straight feels, rolls, triplets, ratchet bursts and fills, set by COMPLEX, with a memory against repeats.
  • HOME: a two-line helper with larger text and nine pictures in the random rotation.

What stays

64 steps and three patterns per channel, 35 scales, and generation of sequences, sounds, LFOs, effects and parameter locks. Pattern, generator, mute and solo changes start on the next bar. Undo/redo, temporary save, Control All, MIDI and backups.

Drum library

40 factory shots, ten for each category, plus four user banks. The sample manager converts audio, replaces a chosen shot and keeps its category.
