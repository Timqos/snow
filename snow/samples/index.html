Samples

Import and replace shots in the KK, SD, HH and PR categories. On the FM-1, PRESET on HOME 1 (DR) draws new shots in all four categories, and HOME 2 K1–K4 draw new shots for kick, snare, hat and percussion with their new sequence.

Needs Chrome or Edge with Web MIDI SysEx.

Import / replace

New files are added to the bank. “Replace” next to a shot picks that one position and keeps its category.

Conversion: mono, 22 050 Hz, IMA ADPCM, normalised, with a short fade at the end. Four banks of 60 KiB: up to 11 shots of 0.5 s per bank, 44 in all. Longer shots mean fewer. Up to 16 shorter shots per bank. The 40 factory shots are stored separately.

Before writing, the bank is downloaded automatically as a backup file. Writing changes only the chosen user bank. Stop PLAY on the FM-1 and keep USB connected until the read-back check has finished.

Full backup

Projects, patterns, presets, settings and the four user banks. An archive from the old STATTIC on Skiff can only be restored in the old firmware.
