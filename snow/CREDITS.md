STATTIC 0.9.0-S1 is built on SLOOP 2.8.5 by isod89, commit
bac9c05ea7627449405aa10e48da21d75b6c8c56. SLOOP builds on Felucca by Leo
Kuroshita (@kurogedelic), Hügelton Instruments, and includes the dual-core work
credited to Andre Rezemini. Original source notices and third-party licences
remain in the source archive. The firmware and these adaptations are GPL-3.0-only.

STATTIC synthesis, presets, independent effect racks, performance functions and
reference HOME artwork were ported from Timqos/snow b0cb3a5. The five HOME design
references and brand mark were supplied by the project owner. Factory drum shots
are rendered from the STATTIC presets, rather than using SLOOP's stock sample bank.

The installer/package/legacy-backup modules retain their Felucca/Skiff copyright
notices. The sample conversion code is adapted from the SLOOP web editor. The
Fukiai web font's notice is in FONT-LICENSE.txt. FM6 and the other upstream
components keep their original licences in the source tree, including components
not selectable in the STATTIC personality. Names and marks belong to their owners.
