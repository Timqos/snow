A custom six-channel groovebox firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. Kick, snare, hat, pitched percussion, bass and chord — deep synthesis, independent effects and an instrument-aware generative sequencer.

Updated in 0.7.1: clearer HOME, moving pixel details. SELECT switches synthesis, generation and channel mix. Four generator knobs give direct access to sounds, phrases with locks, LFOs and FX. Larger channel and pattern badges, smaller channel artwork and binary pixel animation make HOME easier to read. Eyes, ears, globe details, arrows and envelopes move without a shadow effect; the cassette is unchanged.

One role. Many voices.

KICKSNAREHATPERCBASSCHORD

Ten factory sounds per instrument, three oscillators, six algorithms, full ADSR, pitch/FM envelopes, filters and two LFOs. Per-channel delay, reverb, chorus, Tape, bitcrush and insert effects. MIXER and PATTERNS show all six channels together. The pixel interface links icon animation directly to parameter position.

Try it in your browser.

Open the simulator and click Switch on. HOME → SELECT right activates generation: K1 sound, K2 phrase + locks, K3 LFO, K4 FX. Left arrows activate synth macros; up arrows activate the channel mix. Each generator detent creates another result; hold SAVE to undo. The updated quick-start and button manual sit below the device. Browser saves remain local.

Install with a way back.

Snow 0.7.1 is experimental FM-1 firmware and has not yet been validated on physical hardware. Install and use it at your own risk and responsibility, subject to applicable law. Read the risk and responsibility notice and matching-factory return guide. Back up your music and keep the current firmware before installing.
