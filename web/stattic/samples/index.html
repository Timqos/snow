DR / SAMPLE LIBRARY

Samples

Your own drum sounds on the FM-1, in four banks: USR 1–4, up to 16 sounds each. DR synthesises its own micro drums; your sounds join its draws now and then (one in four).

  1. Press STOP on the FM-1, then Connect.
  2. On a bank press + Add sounds and choose files. They go onto the FM-1 straight away.
  3. ✕ deletes a sound from the FM-1, ▶ plays it here.

Not connected. Use Chrome or Edge.

FM-1 not found?

Choose its MIDI ports. They may carry an older name, such as Felucca or SLOOP: the computer remembers the first name it saw.
