DR / SAMPLE LIBRARY

Samples

Put your own drum sounds on the FM-1. It has four user banks, USR 1–4. Each holds up to 16 sounds and about 59 KiB. Every sound has a category: KK kick, SD snare, HH hat or PR percussion. DR plays it on the pads of that category, alongside the 40 factory sounds. PRESET on HOME 1 and HOME 2 K1–K4 pick from both.

1Connect the FM-1

Use Chrome or Edge. Press STOP on the FM-1 and close other MIDI applications.

Not connected.

The FM-1 is not found? Choose its MIDI ports

The FM-1’s ports can carry an older name, such as Felucca or SLOOP: your computer remembers the name it first saw.

2Your banks

What is on the FM-1 now. Changes you make here stay on this page until you press Save to FM-1 on that bank; a bank with unsaved changes is outlined in orange.

3Add sounds

WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3, several at once. Each file becomes one sound: mono, 22 050 Hz, IMA ADPCM, its level normalised and a short fade at its end; anything longer than “Longest sound” is cut there. Then press Save to FM-1 on the bank.

4Full backup

Projects, patterns, presets, settings and the four user banks. An archive from the old STATTIC on Skiff can only be restored in the old firmware.
