STATTIC

Four instruments on the M-VAVE FM-1 for ambient IDM: synthesised micro drums, an FM bass and two FM keys voices. STATTIC generators for sequences, sounds, effects and parameter locks, and a six-page HOME with a 1-bit night scene that plays along with the music.

A candidate for testing on hardware. Builds and host tests cannot prove click-free audio on a particular FM-1. The USB console shows each channel's processing time and a counter of missed audio buffers.

In this build

  • AMBIENT generator for BS, SY and CH (EVOLVE → GENERATE; ALL keeps the energetic one): few, long, slow notes, held chords, the bass on the strong beats.
  • SY and CH: HOME 2 K2 draws a style (scape, dark keys, pluck, mallet, bell, blip) with its own reverb and delay; the notes stay in tune and every sound fades out. K3 (LFOs) never touches the pitch. K4 draws delay and reverb up to about 90 %, with long tails. SY also has five 8-bit / FX sounds, one K2 turn in four.
  • DR: synthesised micro drums, every draw new; lighter rhythms in moods (sparse, light, full), euclidean and polymetric figures, short kick retrigs in triplet grids.
  • HOME 1–3: one 1-bit scene: a man waters the garden in time with the music, four flowers flash with the channels, stars sparkle on the hats; the knobs call a UFO, a planet, an astronaut, a comet; the house's dish sends the turns into the sky.
  • HOME 6 PROJECTS: save, open and erase the slots A–D with the knobs.
  • Every other window in HOME's style.

What stays

64 steps and three patterns per channel, 35 scales, and generation of sequences, sounds, LFOs, effects and parameter locks. Pattern, generator, mute and solo changes start on the next bar. Undo/redo, temporary save, Control All, MIDI and backups.

Drums and samples

DR synthesises its drums: 42 families of short clicks, pops, ticks and pings, varied anew with every draw. Four user banks hold your own samples, which join the draws now and then; the sample manager adds and deletes them straight on the FM-1. The previous build with 40 sampled factory shots is kept in the archive (see the manual).
